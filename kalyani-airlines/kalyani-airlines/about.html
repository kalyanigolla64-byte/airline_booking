ABOUT THE OWNER

Kalyani

Founder & Owner, Kalyani Airlines

Kalyani is the founder and owner of Kalyani Airlines, a fictional airline created as a B.Tech web-development project. Her vision is to build a modern, passenger-friendly airline experience where technology, comfort and simple service come together.

She believes that every journey should begin with confidence and end with a memorable experience. Her goal is to connect travelers with beautiful destinations while making flight booking easy and enjoyable.
